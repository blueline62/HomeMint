What we cover

Everything behind the number.

A price with no explanation is just an opinion. Here's what your agent walks you through on the call.

A defended price range

A low, a likely, and a high — and the conditions that would push your home toward each end.

The comparable sales

The actual addresses that sold near you, what they went for, and why yours sits above or below them.

Timing you can act on

How long homes like yours are sitting, and whether waiting a season is likely to help or cost you.

Repairs worth making

The short list of fixes that tend to return more than they cost in your market — and the ones that don't.

Block-level detail

Schools, new listings, and the small geography that moves prices two streets over but not on yours.

Your questions, answered

The advantage of a call over a report: you can push back, ask about your specific situation, and get an answer right then.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no fee and no card. Agents offer these calls because some homeowners eventually list with them — but you're under no obligation to, and plenty of people who book one aren't selling yet.

How long is the call?

Usually 15 to 20 minutes. Long enough to cover your home's value and the reasoning behind it, and to answer whatever you want to ask. If you're short on time, say so at the start and your agent will keep it to the essentials.

When will they call, and can I choose the time?

You choose it. The form asks for the day and time that works for you, and your agent calls then. If the slot you picked stops working, reply to your confirmation email and we'll move it.

Is this a sales pitch?

It's a conversation about your home's value. If you're thinking about selling, your agent can explain what that would look like — but only if you ask. Say you're just curious and the call stays exactly that.

How is this different from an online estimate?

Automated estimates work from public records and can't see your kitchen, your street noise, or the extension you added in 2019. A local agent adjusts for all of it and can tell you, out loud, where they landed and why.

Is this a formal appraisal?

No. An appraisal is a licensed, fee-based assessment usually ordered by a lender. This is an agent's professional opinion of market value, given over the phone. It isn't a substitute for an appraisal in a mortgage or legal context.

What happens to my information?

It goes to the agent who handles your area so they can prepare for the call and reach you at the time you picked. We don't sell contact details to third parties. Ask us to delete your record any time and we will.

I'm not selling for a couple of years. Worth booking?

It's often the best time. Knowing your range early tells you which improvements are worth making and which aren't, well before you're on a deadline.
